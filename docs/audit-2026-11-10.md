# Audit sprint, 2026-11-10

A deep-dive audit of Eluate 0.1.0-beta (commit fb082d1) and the fixes that came out of it.
Ten independent, evidence-based reviews ran in parallel on a fresh clone, each on a fixed
scope, each required to back every finding with a measurement, a failing test, a derivation
or a cited standard. Their full reports are in [`docs/audit-2026-11-10/`](audit-2026-11-10/).
All measurements used synthetic signals (no third-party audio); the real-material tests
skipped as designed.

Environment: Windows 11, Python 3.12.13 (uv-managed venv from `requirements/lock-win-x64-py312.txt`),
numpy 2.5.4, scipy 1.18.1, librosa 1.0.0, Rust 1.96, Node 26. Baseline before any change:
fast tier 1004 passed / 72 skipped; full suite 1077 passed / 0 failed / 75 skipped;
`cargo test --locked` 16 passed; clippy clean in shipped code.

## 1. The question the maintainer asked first: audio-rate automation

Earlier versions had an audible defect from per-sample parameter automation. The audit
([audio-rate.md](audit-2026-11-10/audio-rate.md)) drove every dynamic stage the way the planner
drives it, at all five characters, with a re-runnable harness, and measured the gain signal and
the output.

What is **not** a problem any more:

- The 1 ms control grid and its PCHIP interpolation. The knot lines at multiples of 1 kHz sit
  87 to 135 dB below the carrier; less than 0.1 % of the gain signal's energy lies above 500 Hz;
  the ±1 kHz sidebands on a probe tone are at or below −67 dB. There is no zipper noise.
- The whole-signal clipper: span clipping is identical to the whole-signal curve (difference 0),
  the band-limited change has no energy above Nyquist, aliasing residue ≤ −84 dB.
- The 44.1 kHz control grid (44.1 samples per ms): zero boundary error, identical distortion to 48 kHz.
- M/S linking's audio-rate minimum (live profile): slope kinks exist but the output difference is
  −77 to −94 dB.

What **is** still audio-rate automation, ranked by audibility, with the measured numbers:

| # | Stage | Mechanism | Measured (before fix) | Characters affected |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Glue (FET-style) | 0.1–0.8 ms attacks collapse to one 1 ms control step, applied full-band including the sub | fall of up to 4.05 dB in 1 ms (5.7 dB/ms); sub × gain energy in 100–1000 Hz −40 dB re the sub; onset 1–5 kHz energy +21 dB; steady-sub THD −35/−38 dB (30/40 Hz) | fast, aggressive (moderate less; live profile unaffected at 20 ms) |
| 2 | Final 8x limiter | sample-peak detector, short attack, exponential release: the gain rides inside each sub cycle | 3 dB GR on a 30/40 Hz sine → ripple 0.49/0.37 dB, THD −35/−37 dB (odd harmonics) at fast/aggressive; −38/−41 moderate; −48/−54 transparent | all but transparent |
| 3 | Band ceilings and the frequency-dependent limiter | 2–6 ms lookahead, 50 ms default release on the sub band (the planner never set a per-band release) | 6 dB cut on 30–40 Hz → 0.48/0.18 dB ripple, THD −39/−48 dB | aggressive only |
| 4 | Multiband compressor, sub band | as 2, but the hull fills the ±4 ms required-gain hump | 0.22/0.11 dB ripple, THD −45/−51 dB | aggressive only |
| 5 | Notch depth envelopes | the planner quantised depth to 0.5 dB steps at 1 ms | modulation sidebands −47 dB instead of −64 dB | all (inaudible, pointless) |

The architect's hypothesis (ripple ≈ (1 − exp(−(T/2 − attack/2)/release)) · depth) was accurate
for the limiter and overestimated the compressor and ceilings by 2–3×, because the release hull
fills the required-gain hump. The distortion is odd-order (H3), not the even-order the
interpolation theory predicted.

Fixes (section 3) remove items 1–5: a hold window in the envelope smoother (one period of the
lowest frequency of interest, keeping the proven `g ≤ g_req` bound), per-band detector windows
and releases for the band ceilings, a 3 ms floor on the glue's control-rate attack, and
unquantised notch envelopes. Prototyped cost: the loudness target is still met; limiter GR moves
by 0.1–0.3 dB; sub-band thresholds shift 0.6–1.3 dB at the same meter reading.

### 2.1 Filter maths and stage alignment

Every RBJ and shelf coefficient matches the cookbook and the prewarped analog prototype; the
band-shelf identity, the notch width, the composite clamp, the side strip's mono compatibility,
the resampler (±0.0089 dB ripple, −60.9 dB at Nyquist, zero phase), the overlap-add streaming
(bit-identical to scipy), the band sum (4.4e-16) and float64 throughout all verify. Every stage
has zero broadband latency, so the A/B files and the stems null test are safe.

The one S1 is the **zero-phase lowest crossover**: once the sub band is gained (which every
multiband hit does), the 100 Hz zero-phase crossover pre-rings the kick. Measured on a synthetic
kick after silence at 48 kHz: sub band −6 dB → pre-echo −14.9 dB re the kick peak, above −40 dB
6.3 ms before the onset; −3 dB → −19.6 dB. That is 6 dB louder than the zero-phase high-pass
pre-echo the maintainer removed in October (R7e). A Linkwitz-Riley (minimum-phase) lowest
crossover gives exactly zero pre-onset output and a flat allpass sum; the spec named it the
expected winner but the planner never selected it. Section 3 adds it as `--xover-phase minimum`;
zero phase stays the default (the maintainer's blind-test pick, and section 3.11's peak regrowth).

Also: notch band edges for merging use analog geometric edges that differ from the realised
warped ones above fs/4 (S2); `math.atanh` raises for high-f0/low-Q notches reachable through
merging (S3); IIR tails in digital silence run subnormal and slow the band split 9–21× (S4).

### 2.2 Planner and solver

All budget objectives are monotone (0 violations over about 260 sweeps), solves hit their targets
to 1e-4 dB, plans are identical at 1, 4 and 12 threads, renders are bit-identical across threads
and repeats, the plan round trip and version checks hold. The S3s are robustness: any input under
1 s or silent runs the whole analysis then dies on a NaN level record; 22.05 kHz input crashes in
the side-shelf fit; the loud-hits fallback ignores the −70 dBFS gate and can solve thresholds at
−800 dBFS on a near-silent tail. S2: a sparse band's threshold step-down can run 48 dB into the
band's leakage floor with the gain pinned at the clamp and no warning; the clipper's rendered
reduction compares unlike peaks; a wrong `--bpm` is never surfaced. The documented input-level
invariance (one 24-bit step) is overstated: real files at −20 and +6 dB differ by 658 steps
(−82 dBFS) because an EQ corner rounds differently. The base-rate re-limit touched 98 % of samples
at 0.29 dB because the 8x limiter output read 0.011 dB over the ceiling before it.

### 2.3 Analysis features

Six S1s, all of the kind that silently steers the plan: beatless material gets a beat grid marked
informative (an ambient pad read 135–175 BPM, pink noise 113.5), so random beats become the plan's
hits and set the character; key-locked crossovers follow a key that has no confidence check (pink
noise read C minor at 0.29); common minor progressions read as the relative major, moving the low
crossover from 110 to 80–87 Hz; a true 140 BPM track can flip to "fast" on estimation noise at the
strict boundary; kicks are lost under sustained bass and pad (20 of 43 found) and the detector's
recall depends on the sample rate; the beat grid lands 17–22 ms late without four-on-the-floor
kicks. S2: side-only resonances are detected but never cut; hiss above −60 dBFS is not flagged at
all; false notches on dense material; a hard-panned band reads fully mono. The spec's own gates
all pass (tilt −3.003 dB/oct on pink noise, the 7 kHz ring at −0.04 %, bass root 32/32,
segmentation at 0 bars error, SuperFlux precision/recall 1.0, BS.468 within 0.09 dB). The
borrowed onset code is the maintainer's own and AGPL-compatible; its header said GPL.

### 2.4 Stems

Three S1s in the correction pass: a notch the planner flagged musical (cap 0 dB, "do not cut")
is cut on the stem anyway; the bass role goes to any stem with 40 % of its energy below 150 Hz,
so a three-stem delivery's "music" stem is ducked under the drums; a bass already sidechained 6 dB
is ducked a further 2.2 dB. The loop's limit accounting cannot act on the loudness shortfall that
dominates its own score, counts planner-rejected notches as limits and fixes them, and applies EQ
steps the planner did not want. The null test refuses polarity-inverted, limited or
half-sample-offset references instead of diagnosing them, searches only ±10 ms, and crashes on an
exact null. Measured: six 30 s stems took 38 min at two threads, with the stems-specific work
about 230 s of it.

### 2.5 Live profile

The profile's budgets, timing, M/S link and ISO-226 weighting measure as intended (averages on
budget, no pumping, no cymbal cuts, no false kicks on upright bass), and the electronic profile is
clearly wrong on the same material (crest 9.3–10 dB). Three S1 behaviours: auto character above
140 BPM turns the clipper on under live (a rock band was clipped while the limiter did 0 dB); nine
dynamic notches were rendered on the partials of a vibrato string ensemble; the input EQ rotates
tone by up to 2.5 dB at Tilt 0 and the tilt check is structurally dead under live. The
uncalibrated −10 dBFS target lands masters at −12.5 to −13.6 LUFS integrated, above every
platform's normalisation point, costing 2.6–4.0 dB of crest for nothing. The calibration proposal
(target −11.5, clipper off unless a character is chosen, conditional side strip, identity EQ at
Tilt 0, a measured "sustained material" rule, live flags for PSR/PLR/LRA/crest) is in the report
with its evidence tables; see section 3 for what was adopted.

### 2.6 Metering and delivery

The measurement core is correct: K-weighting matches BS.1770-5 at six sample rates (≤ 0.0075 dB),
pyloudnorm agreement ±0.004 LU, the EBU Tech 3341/3342 cases rebuilt synthetically pass (worst
−0.022 LU), the Annex 2 true-peak coefficients are bit-exact, TPDF dither is exactly ±1 LSB,
and the ceiling holds on 4x, 8x, 32x and an independent 64x reference after quantisation. What
was missing is delivery information: no integrated LUFS, platform playback gain or codec-headroom
check anywhere; the UI level-matched the premaster 0.6–0.7 dB differently from the A/B files the
user hears; the UI showed the 8x true peak, not the constrained worst-of-three; `--streaming`
meant 16-bit at the input rate. `meter.measure` K-weighted the signal six times (263 s and
+2.8 GB on a 10-minute 96 kHz file).

### 2.7 UI and UX

Reproduced in headless Edge with a synthetic data set: the spectrum's tilt target followed the live
controls rather than the rendered master; "Slow" was shown where the engine renders Moderate;
reopening a mastered folder could silently re-master at default settings, overwriting the old
masters; a double Delete on a rendering row removed two tracks. A master could be re-voted after
its answer was shown; the crossover sliders were unreachable by assistive technology; amber was
the only cue for "short"; the 960×640 minimum and 200 % text overflowed; several engine flags
never showed for a single track and integrated loudness appeared nowhere. The privacy promise
holds: no audio, file names or paths leave the machine.

### 2.8 Desktop shell, privacy, CI, licences

The shell contacts exactly one endpoint, an opt-in consent-gated vote POST, with no telemetry,
update check or install ID. `cargo test --locked` and clippy are clean in shipped code. The
robustness items: a non-UTF-8 byte on engine output stops the reader and hangs the job; a JSON
message that is not an object aborts the app (panic = abort); engines are orphaned if the shell
dies (no Job Object); symlinked audio in a chosen folder is listed then refused. Licensing:
THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt was missing 12 shipped crates including the TLS stack, and no Rust crate
licence texts were shipped; the macOS/Linux packages carry Windows-only notices.

### 2.9 Code hygiene and tests

Full suite green. `eluate master out/master.wav -o out/` overwrote its own input and exited 0;
`eluate report` did not check the premaster's hash; a locked output failed after a 387 s solve;
`setuptools>=68` cannot build the SPDX licence field (77 can); two source files are committed with
CRLF endings and there was no `.gitattributes`; docs/spec-draft.md contradicted the code in ten
places; one analysis run spent 527 s in the clipper bisection (42 whole-track oversampled readings).

## 3. Fixes applied in this sprint

Eight fix branches, one per area (the live profile's calibration came after them, 3.9), each
merged into `audit/2026-11-10` with `--no-ff` so `git log --first-parent` lists one merge per
area. Every change that alters the sound for new plans is a plan field; a plan saved before
renders bit for bit as before (`test_old_style_plan_renders_bit_identically_to_before`, against
fb082d1), except ms_link plans (< −70 dB). The detail is in the documents each area updated:
[gain-staging.md](gain-staging.md) §15 item 6 and §21, [spec-draft.md](spec-draft.md) 3.3,
[noise-and-stems.md](noise-and-stems.md) §2 addendum and the UI docs.

Five branches (features, planner, DSP, stems, metering) were interrupted by usage limits
partway through; their state was committed as "WIP: … (snapshot at hand-over)" and finished
afterwards, so those commits are part of the fixes rather than throwaway work.

### 3.1 Audio-rate automation and filter maths (section 1, 2.1)

Items 1–5 of section 1, and the lowest crossover. Measured with the harness now in
[`tools/audio_rate/`](../tools/audio_rate/), h1 to h7 re-run on the merged tree (before: the
audit's run on fb082d1; more rows in gain-staging §21):

| what | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| final limiter, 3 dB GR, aggressive, 30 Hz: ripple / THD | 0.49 dB / −34.9 dB | 0.00 dB / −183 dB |
| sub-band compressor, aggressive, 30 Hz: ripple / THD | 0.22 dB / −44.7 dB | 0.00 dB / −205 dB |
| band ceiling 6 dB, aggressive, 30 Hz: ripple / THD | 0.48 dB / −39.3 dB | 0.00 dB / −207 dB |
| glue, sub + kicks, aggressive: largest 1 ms gain fall | 4.05 dB | 1.39 dB |
| glue, aggressive, onset energy 1–5 kHz re the input's (h4) | +21.1 dB | +4.2 dB |
| glue, aggressive, sub × gain in 100–1000 Hz re the sub (h4) | −40.2 dB | −46.0 dB |
| 100 Hz crossover, kick after silence, sub −6 dB: pre-ring (gain-staging §21) | −14.9 dB re kick, 6.3 ms over −40 dB | none with `--xover-phase minimum`; unchanged by default |
| planner notch envelopes, no kick mask: worst tone sideband / largest 1 ms step (h5) | −47.4 dB / 0.5 dB | −63.0 dB / 0.006 dB |

How: a hold in `envelope.box_smooth` (25 ms on the final limiter, keeping `g ≤ g_req`);
band detectors that read one period of the band's lower edge, with per-band ceiling releases;
a 3 ms floor on the glue's control-rate attack; the M/S link's minimum taken on the control
grid; unquantised notch envelopes, with the composite clamp's own steps smoothed; a
Linkwitz-Riley **minimum-phase lowest crossover as a toggle** (`--xover-phase minimum`; zero
phase stays the default, see 3.11). Also: notches merge on their realised (bilinear-warped)
edges, the zero-phase pad follows the slowest real or complex pole (no domain error), and IIR
tails in digital silence are flushed (band split 1.06 → 0.33 s on a padded 20 s file).

**Not fixed:** the glue's steady-sub distortion is lower but still there (aggressive 30 / 40 /
60 Hz THD −38.8 / −42.6 / −48.5 dB, was −35.3 / −38.3 / −44.9; transparent −56 dB). Two
prototypes that address it (a trailing hold on the glue's required gain, the glue above a
split only) are in `tools/audio_rate/harness/fixes.py`, measured, not adopted. The base-rate
re-limit still touches 93 % of samples on the 30 s loop (max 0.39 dB); a 0.4 dB aim margin
would get it under 1 % at −0.18 dB RMS, not done (gain-staging §21 item 3).

### 3.2 Planner and solver (2.2)

Inputs under 1 s or with no gated window are refused before the analysis (`cli.check_input`,
`check_audio`), and the plan's measured level is None rather than NaN; the 22.05 kHz side-shelf
crash is fixed; a hit below the −70 dBFS gate is never "loud", and with no loud hit the budget
stages leave the audio alone with a warning. A sparse band's threshold search now stops at a
floor 12 dB under the band's body (its median 1 s RMS at the loud hits) and reports "target unreachable"
instead of running 48 dB into its leakage floor and saying "ok". The clipper's planned and
rendered falls are read on the same signal (they agree to 1e-9 dB); a user `--bpm` is recorded
with the detected tempo beside it. The level-invariance claim is now measured and stated as
it is: separately rounded files at 0, −20 and +6 dB get the identical EQ and master to within
one 24-bit step on 5 % of samples (was 658 steps). The limiter's aim margin is a plan field.

### 3.3 Analysis features (2.3)

Beatless material no longer gets an informative grid (grid evidence is required; every
detected constant grid is refitted); key-locked crossovers need a key confidence of 0.5
(pink noise falls back to the default crossovers and says why); relative major/minor ties are
broken by the bass; the adaptive kick gate is capped and onset refinement scales with the
sample rate; loud hiss reads from a noise plateau instead of −inf; dense material's false notches
(a supersaw's 5th and 7th partials, a one-sided baseline at 19 kHz) get the new `harmonic` and
`hf_exempt` flags; padded frames at the file's edges no longer put a resonance at 0 s. The
analysis cache key now includes a hash of the analysis sources, so a code change invalidates
caches even when the version bump is forgotten. New fixtures: drum loops, a groove track, a
beatless pad, a held chord and a dense loop.

### 3.4 Stems (2.4)

The correction pass honours the planner's zero or rejected notch caps and sizes every EQ step
by the planner's wanted gain; a three-stem "music" stem no longer takes the bass role; an
existing sidechain is read past the note's attack (the audit's 6 dB / 100 ms ducked sub now gets
no further duck, was 2.2 dB more), and a stem whose removal does not lower the sum's peak is
never compressed for it; the shortfall that dominates the score is reported as such. The null
test diagnoses polarity, ±250 ms lags and sub-sample offsets by name instead of refusing, and an
exact null serialises (−200 dB). Stem paths are checked (missing, duplicate, one basename twice,
a file the run itself writes) before the memory guard, which is now stem-aware and recorded in
run.json. Spectra and notch readings are one pass per stem: attribution on the audit's
6 × 30 s set took 35.0 s, now 4.5 s. `--cd` with `--stems` is refused for now.

### 3.5 Metering and delivery (2.6)

`--streaming` is now −1 dBTP at 24 bits (16-bit only for `--cd`, which resamples to 44.1 kHz with
TPDF dither); the report and UI show the worst-of-three true peak, integrated LUFS and the
delivery data, and the UI's level match is the A/B files' own. `meter.measure` K-weights once
(the 10-minute 96 kHz case no longer takes 263 s and 2.8 GB extra).

### 3.6 UI and UX (2.7)

The spectrum's tilt target follows the rendered master; the character shown is the one
rendered; reopening a mastered folder can no longer silently re-master over it; double Delete,
re-voting after the answer, the unreachable crossover sliders, colour-only "short" cues and
the minimum-size / 200 % text overflow are fixed; every engine flag and integrated loudness
are shown; the consent text names every shared field and sharing reads off only once the
outbox is cleared.

### 3.7 Desktop shell, privacy, CI, licences (2.8)

Engine pipes survive non-UTF-8 bytes and non-object JSON; engines live in a Job Object, so they
die with the shell; chosen folders use canonical paths. The vote payload is whitelisted on the
wire. THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES now lists the current crate graph with the Rust licence texts. CI tests
`main` on push, runs clippy with `-D warnings` and checks the notices are fresh.

### 3.8 Code hygiene and tests (2.9)

`eluate master out/master.wav -o out/` (and the stems equivalent) is refused before anything is
touched; every per-run product is cleared at the start, and `report` checks run.json against the
plan and the premaster's SHA-256; a locked output is found before the solve; packaging needs
setuptools ≥ 77; `.gitattributes` stores text as LF (three CRLF files renormalised);
spec-draft.md carries a dated "historical" status block; the slow marker means over 20 s.

### 3.9 Live profile (2.5)

The calibration proposal (live.md §B.4) was approved by the maintainer and applied as written;
details and deviations in live.md "Applied 2026-11-10" and gain-staging §22. `calibrated` stays
False: the numbers rest on synthetic fixtures and published statistics until the maintainer's
own live masters are measured, and the report says so in those words.

- Target −11.5 on the meter (was −10; about −14.5 LUFS integrated on band material).
- The clipper is never on under auto; only when the user chooses fast or aggressive (A1).
- Notches only on "resonance" candidates, never "musical" ones (A2).
- Input EQ bounds ±1 dB; at Tilt 0 the high-pass and one narrow bell (excess > 4 dB, Q ≥ 2)
  only, no shelves on M or S (A3).
- Side strip at 60 Hz only when the sub band's decoherence is at least 0.3, else none (A10).
- Sustained material (onsets/s < 0.5): multiband 0.5 / 0.75 / 0.75 / 1.0 dB, glue 0.5, limiter
  1.0 / 2.0, no frequency-dependent limiting, no notches, 400 ms hits ("no beat grid: sustained
  material") unless the user gave `--bpm`; recorded in plan_record["sustained"].
- Live-only report checks: PSR p95 < 10 (amber < 8), PLR < 12, LRA drop > 3 LU when LRA > 12,
  crest drop > 3 dB, B.4's loudness sanity ranges, the slope against −4.5 dB/oct (informational),
  and provenance flags for clipper-on, notches on tonal material and no beat grid (A5, A7).
- `--target-rms` help names both defaults; the UI's live default is −11.5.

Electronic and stems plans, records, targets and a render are bit-identical before and after
(checked on two synthetic tracks at default, fast, transparent, Tilt +1 and stems). Not done:
A4 (the M/S-linked report, open question D.8 in live.md), A6's grid rule for every profile, A9,
A11, A13, and A10's alternative band-0 correlation test; the auto character on sustained
material can still quote a detected tempo the music does not follow (A14).

### 3.10 Merging notes, for the next review

- The planner branch's first threshold floor sat 12 dB under the band's loudest-section p95. In a

  every gated 1 s window, which the final audit found follows a quiet intro instead (12 s of

  fixture).
- Where branches met: notches merge per channel *and* on realised edges (an S notch is still kept
  out of a stereo notch's band, on realised edges); the threshold search takes both the floor and
  the band's detector window; speclim takes both the no-loud-hits guard and the crossover phase;
  `cli` keeps the `--cd` resampling, the analysis-code cache key and the input checks together.
- Tests the branches had not caught up with were moved, not loosened: new result keys
  (`floor_db`, `bound`), `report.json` now cleared at the start, the stems fake `do_report` taking
  `check_run`, and the "unknown key confidence" case, which needed the fake key to drop the
  confidence now that `pitch.key` provides one.
- Fast suite on the merged tree: 1202 passed before the live calibration, 1210 passed after (72 skipped, 0 failed either time).
- Seen during the calibration, not fixed: the UI draws the "Side" handle at a 100 Hz fallback

  On this machine `tests/test_planner_fixes.py`, run as a whole file, once crashed with an access

### 3.11 Final audit (after the merges)

A second review of the merged branch: the whole diff scanned for leaked paths and binaries
(none), the slow tier and the desktop shell run for the first time since the merges, and the
riskiest new code measured rather than read.

- **Crossover default put back to zero phase.** The slow tier had one failure,
  `test_solve_meets_the_rms_target_when_the_budgets_suffice`: the dense fixture's master ended
  1.7 dB short of −4.1 with the limiter at its budget, where zero phase reaches it with no
  limiter GR at all. Cause: the minimum-phase band sum is an allpass, and an allpass un-flattens
  a clipped waveform. With no gain applied, the true peak moved by:

  | material | minimum-phase band sum, true peak |
  |---|---|
  | dense fixture (55 Hz stack + pink through a tanh, crest 4.8 dB) | +5.85 dB |
  | dense loop, clipped 6 dB | +4.81 dB |
  | sectioned track, clipped 6 dB | +2.09 dB |
  | groove track, clipped 6 dB | +1.59 dB |
  | the same three unclipped (crest 14 to 15.5 dB) | −0.81, −0.37, +1.80 dB |

  The DSP branch had measured the cost only on an unclipped loop (0.05 dB). Loud electronic
  premasters often arrive clipped or limited, so the default went back to the released
  behaviour, which is also the maintainer's pick from blind tests; minimum phase remains as
  `--xover-phase minimum`. Synthetic signals only: no real premaster was measured.
- **Threshold floor re-anchored** (3.10, first bullet): the merge's median anchor reopened the
  S2-3 descent on a track with a quiet intro; the floor now reads the band at the loud hits.
- **Resonance recall on dense material is low, and was before the sprint.** A +9 dB, Q 12
  resonance injected into the dense loop at eight frequencies off any played partial: one
  (2500 Hz) is flagged for a full cut, at fb082d1 and now alike; the rest read "tonal" or are
  not detected, because the ring lands on the supersaw's line spectrum. The new `harmonic`
  flag cost one of them (5300 Hz, half of which sits on a detuned saw's 8th partial) its
  quarter-depth "musical" cut, against three false notches removed. Not a regression; a limit
  of the detector on line-spectrum-dense material worth knowing.
- **Live profile, small points, not changed:** the loudness sanity range assumes the default
  −11.5 target and reads "outside" for any other `--target-rms`; the `notches_tonal` flag
  cannot fire now that live never renders a "musical" candidate; at Tilt 0 the input EQ is the

## 4. Open questions for the maintainer

1. Lowest crossover phase: settled. Zero phase stays the default (the maintainer's blind-test
   pick); minimum phase is the `--xover-phase minimum` toggle. See 3.11 for what it costs on
   clipped material.
